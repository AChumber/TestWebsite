News

What has been happening at Northwood

Highlights from our first ever newsletter, October 2026.

New school year

Welcome to a new school year

Our first week has been filled with exciting activities and new discoveries. We started with the "All About Me" theme, where students shared their favourite things and created colourful posters about themselves. These posters are now proudly displayed in our classroom.

Wellbeing

Mental Health Awareness Day, 10 October

To bring awareness to mental wellbeing we completed "writing for wellbeing" activities while opening up conversations about what we can do to help our wellbeing. This ties in nicely with the National Year of Reading. Let us be kind to ourselves this month.

Fundraising

Macmillan Coffee Morning

We are raising money for Macmillan Cancer Support. If you would like to donate, please contact the school office.

Online safety

Staying safe online over half term

With the half-term holidays approaching, children may spend more time online. Set parental controls on devices, talk to your child about what they are watching and who they are talking to, and know the age ratings for apps and games. For more support, visit the NSPCC online safety pages. If something online worries you, you can report it to the CEOP Safety Centre, or talk to us.
