Our uniform

Simple, smart and the same for everyone

A uniform helps every pupil feel that they belong and that they are part of one school community. We keep it simple and practical so that getting ready for school is as calm as possible.

Everyday uniform

  • A white polo shirt or school shirt
  • Black trousers
  • A plain black sweatshirt, jumper or cardigan. Hoodies are not part of the uniform
  • Smart, plain black footwear. Please no heels or flip flops

P.E. kit

  • Plain black P.E. shorts, jogging bottoms or a tracksuit
  • A plain white t-shirt
  • Plimsolls or training shoes

Bags and belongings

  • Bags should hold only a pencil case, the FME planner, P.E. kit and sanitary products if needed
  • Please do not bring money, personal items or jewellery into school
  • Food and drink from home are not needed, unless we have agreed it with you
  • We are a mobile phone free school. Any phone brought onto site is stored in a pouch at reception
Questions about uniform? Please ask the school office before you buy anything, and we will help. Call 0121 835 1360 or contact us.

School meals

Healthy food through the school day

Eating well helps pupils feel settled, focused and ready to learn. Every pupil has a healthy start to the day, something fresh at break time and a proper cooked lunch.

On arrival

Breakfast club

Every pupil starts the day with breakfast in our Arrival and Breakfast Club from 8.45am, so they are ready to learn from the first lesson.

At break time

Fresh fruit

Fresh fruit is available to every pupil at break time, to keep energy and concentration up through the morning.

At lunchtime

A cooked lunch

Every pupil is offered a healthy, balanced and nutritious cooked lunch, brought in fresh from our local catering partners and included in the school day.

For every pupil

Dietary and allergy needs

We cater for dietary and allergy needs. Please tell the school office about any needs before your child starts, so that we can plan meals with you.

Mobile phones

We are a mobile phone free school

In line with DfE guidance

Following Department for Education guidance, our school day is free from mobile phones, so that pupils can focus on learning, on each other and on feeling settled and safe.

If a pupil brings a phone

Any phone brought onto site is stored in a special pouch at reception, to avoid disruption to learning. Pupils can collect it at the end of the day. Phones must be agreed with the Headteacher first.

Read our policy

The full details are in our Mobile Phone Policy (FME-BE-05). Find it in the Behaviour and pupil conduct policies. If you need to reach your child during the school day, please ring the school office on 0121 835 1360.

What every pupil receives

Given to every pupil when they start

Issued on admission

Your own FME planner

Every pupil is given a branded FME planner when they are admitted. It helps pupils organise their week and keeps school and home in touch with each other.

Issued to every pupil

A Chromebook for learning

Every pupil is issued with a Chromebook to support their learning in school. It is part of how we help pupils become digitally literate citizens.

Provided by the school

All your equipment

We provide all equipment, including stationery, so pupils arrive with only what is on the uniform and bag lists.
