Information for parents, carers and inspectors

About the school

Futures Matter - Northwood is a DfE-registered independent special school for pupils aged 11 to 16 (URN 152552), inspected by Ofsted. Parents and carers can ask the office for any document listed here, free of charge.

Inspection reports

Ofsted pre-registration inspection report, 13 May 2026 (opens on the Ofsted website). The report concludes that the school is likely to meet all the independent school standards if it is registered. We will publish every later inspection report here.

Anti-bullying strategy

We are a 'telling school': every report is listened to, taken seriously and responded to promptly, and all incidents are recorded, monitored and reviewed. Read the Anti-Bullying Policy (FME-BE-02), listed below.

Academic performance and public examination results

Because only a small number of pupils sit public examinations each year, results are not published on our website, to avoid identifying any individual. Results are reported to parents and carers by personal letter on results day, and every parent and carer receives an annual report of their child's progress and attainment.

Complaints procedure and number of complaints

Informal complaints are addressed with a pupil's tutor or class teacher. Formal complaints go to the Headteacher (or the Proprietor, if the complaint is about the Headteacher). Read the Complaints Policy (FME-GD-01), listed below. Formal complaints registered under the formal procedure in the previous school year: none. The school has only just opened, so no complaints have been received.

Removal from the register and restrictions on the proprietor

Neither the Secretary of State nor a justice of the peace has made a decision to remove the school from the register or to restrict the proprietor.

Paper copies and viewing in school

Every document on this page is available to read at the school office during the school day. Parents and carers, including those of prospective pupils, can also ask the school office for a free copy of any document, on paper or by email.

Requesting alternative formats

If you need information in an alternative format, such as an accessible PDF or an audio recording, please email us at office@futuresmattereducation.co.uk and tell us:

  • the web address (URL) of the content you need
  • the details of what you need
  • the format you need
  • your contact name and email address

You can also call the school office on 0121 835 1360. Read our accessibility statement.
