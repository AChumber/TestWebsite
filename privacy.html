Privacy notice

How we use personal information

We hold a lot of personal information, and much of it is sensitive. This page explains what we hold, why, who sees it and what your rights are.

Our website

What this website collects

This website does not use cookies. If you send an enquiry through our contact form, we receive the details you enter (your name, email address, telephone number if given, who you are, the subject and your message). We use them only to reply and to deal with your enquiry, on the basis of our legitimate interests. Enquiries are sent to office@futuresmattereducation.co.uk and are kept in line with our retention schedule. Please do not use the form to report a safeguarding concern: use the safeguarding contact details on the Contact page.

Complaints

Tell the Data Protection Officer first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113, at any time.

Online safety monitoring (Senso, linked to CPOMS) flags activity on school devices for a member of staff to review. No automated decision is made about a pupil. Read the full notices for details. Paper copies are free on request.
